Vulkan Vship is an alternative to vship HIP or vship Cuda, allowing greater portability for intel GPUs or even mobile phone GPUs. It has also been developped to deal with HIP issues that appears for some users like random segfaults/crashes.
Vulkan Vship is currently experimental and not as well optimized
