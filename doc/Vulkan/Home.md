Vulkan Vship is an alternative to vship HIP or vship Cuda, allowing greater portability for intel GPUs or even mobile phone GPUs. It has also been developped to deal with HIP issues that appears for some users like random segfaults/crashes.
Vulkan Vship is currently noticeably slower than the Cuda or HIP backend especially for CVVDP however it is relatively resilient now and serves as a good way to make portable vship librairies/binaries if the best speed is not required.

I recommend this SDK for compilation: https://vulkan.lunarg.com/sdk/home
